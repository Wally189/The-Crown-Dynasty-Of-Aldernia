# Aldernia

Aldernia is a fictional country and participatory editorial world.

This repository is the public-world implementation surface: national front door, institutions, Constitution, economy, public-service model, The Times of Aldernia, Aldernian Life, learning, Quiet Resilience and the clearly separated real-world House of Carol boundary.

## Public promise

Aldernia should be useful, interesting and alive.

It informs, explains, educates, scrutinises, reflects, connects, entertains and remembers. The design language is **1940s grammar · 2026 interface**: civic clarity and confidence translated into a modern, accessible public-service environment.

## Reality boundary

Aldernia is fictional. Its institutions, currency, elections, places, public services and in-world statistics are fictional unless a page clearly identifies real-world material.

It does not claim real sovereignty, public authority, healthcare, banking, accreditation, emergency-service or diplomatic status.

## Electorate

The constitutional electorate is designed but not open. Demo interactions do not transmit or count votes.

## Commercial boundary

House of Carol is a separate real-world business in development. Fictional Aldernian customers, companies, transactions, partnerships or readership do not count as House of Carol market evidence or revenue.
