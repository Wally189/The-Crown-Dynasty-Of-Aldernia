How Aldernia works

Power should have an address.

The Crown, Assembly, Government, independent institutions and specialist Houses have different jobs because useful institutions become dangerous when every job collapses into one.

Crown

King Alan & Queen Barbara

Continuity, constitutional stewardship and ceremonial life. The Crown appoints a Prime Minister who commands Assembly confidence; it does not choose a government because it prefers one.

Assembly

120 elected seats

The legislature passes in-world law, approves budgets and holds Government to account. The founding design uses multi-member regional constituencies and proportional representation.

Government

Ten accountability homes

Cabinet, Treasury, Justice, Home, External Affairs, Health, Education, Economy, Infrastructure and Environment.

Independent institutions

Teach Antony

Electoral integrity, constitutional scrutiny, audit, standards and redress sit outside ordinary Government control.

Media

House of Josie

Independent journalism, broadcasting, explanation and correction. Government may answer stories. It may not edit the finding.

Education

House of Tony

Learning, research, scholarship and public knowledge. Useful education should remain useful even if the reader forgets the fiction entirely.

Health

House of Nathaniel

Health and wellbeing knowledge within professional boundaries. Fictional healthcare never impersonates real clinical care.

Finance

House of Vivienne

Banking, economics and financial literacy. The fictional Aldernian Crown is not real money, an investment or a payment instrument.

Faith

House of Catholic

Catholic theology, formation, history and sacred culture, always distinguishing Aldernian fiction from real Church doctrine and authority.

Enterprise

House of Carol

Business and the transparent boundary into real commercial work. It may commission editorial craft; it cannot commission truth.

NO HOUSE MARKS ITS OWN HOMEWORK.

These institutions are part of Aldernia's fictional public world. Real-world specialist material is identified when used.
