How Aldernia works

Different jobs. Different failure modes.

The Crown, Assembly, Government, courts, watchdogs, specialist Houses and regional government are separated because useful institutions become dangerous when every job collapses into one.

Crown

King Alan & Queen Barbara

Continuity, constitutional stewardship and ceremony. The Crown follows Assembly confidence rather than choosing its preferred Government.

Assembly

120 elected seats

Law, tax, spending, scrutiny and confidence. One chamber at founding, with strong committees and constitutional checks.

Government

Ten accountability homes

Centre, Treasury, Justice, Home, External Affairs, Health, Education, Economy, Infrastructure and Environment.

The Houses

Specialist institutions, not ministries.

Josie · MediaTony · EducationNathaniel · HealthVivienne · FinanceCatholic · FaithCarol · Enterprise

The Houses supply specialist knowledge and services inside the public world. They do not become Government merely because Government needs expertise. Subject Houses may answer journalism, but they do not edit Josie's findings.
