Public services

Eight service families give the public world a practical front door.

These are in-world institutional service systems. They explain how Aldernia is organised; they do not provide real statutory, regulated, clinical, financial or legal services.

Money, tax & public resources

Treasury, Revenue & Customs, public investment and fiscal administration. No real tax filing, banking or regulated financial service.

Justice & legal system

Law, courts administration, legal aid, corrections, independent adjudication and redress. Judicial and prosecutorial merits remain outside ministerial control.

Citizenship, borders & safety

Passport, citizenship, immigration, safeguarding and public-safety institutions as in-world services only.

Health & care

Health-service structure, prevention, health security, medicines, care oversight and evidence. No real clinical service or medical advice.

Education & skills

Schools, children, qualifications, inspection, skills, higher education, student finance, libraries and culture.

Business & innovation

Companies, IP, work, science, innovation and enterprise infrastructure. No real company registration, grant or public finance.

Travel, housing & place

Roads, rail, maritime, licensing, land, planning, housing, mapping and post.

Environment, food & energy

Environment, food safety, biosecurity, nature, marine, meteorology, energy, water and land stewardship.

The Kingdom of Aldernia

Serve well. Speak plainly.

Aldernia is a fictional/simulated constitutional and institutional project. It is not a sovereign state, real public authority or regulated service provider. Real-world outcomes and revenue are separately evidenced.
