Current state

Design, not transaction.

Life events

Thirteen ordinary things a functioning country should handle.

Register a birthCivil registration, then relevant family/health/education information.
Move homeAddress changes, council services and electoral registration option.
Start a businessCompany registration, tax, licences where relevant and finance guidance.
Lose a jobIncome support, employment services and skills routes.
Education & trainingEligibility, support needs, provider status and appeals.
Health & careFictional service navigation with real-world medical boundaries preserved.
Report a local problemPothole, waste, streetlight, park or local nuisance to the responsible council.
Apply for a licenceEligibility, evidence, fee, decision time, reasons and review.
Understand taxLiability, calculation, due date, correction and appeal.
Information & privacyRequest public information or challenge use of personal data.
ComplainService resolution, independent internal review, then Ombudsman where eligible.
Contact representativesFind elected representatives, petition rules and committee status.
VoteDesigned, but deliberately not live until integrity/privacy/accessibility controls exist.

Every service should tell you

  • what it does and who can use it
  • what you need and what it costs
  • what happens next and who owns it
  • how your information is used
  • how to get accessible or non-digital help
  • what to do if it goes wrong
  • how to appeal or complain

Service rule

You should not be bounced because Government reorganised itself.

Cross-department handoffs need an owner, a clear next step, an exception route and a recovery path.

Complaints & redress

1 · Service resolution→2 · Independent internal review→3 · Aldernian Ombudsman

Formal legal-type appeals use the relevant tribunal or court where the fictional scheme provides one. Complaints are treated as evidence for service improvement, not an inconvenience to be minimised.

No live service: Aldernia Services is currently explanatory worldbuilding. It does not collect applications, identity documents, votes, payments or other personal information.
ALDERNIA

A fictional country and participatory editorial world.

Its institutions, currency, elections, places, public services and in-world statistics are fictional unless a page clearly identifies real-world material. Aldernia does not claim real sovereignty, legal authority, healthcare, banking, accreditation, emergency-service or diplomatic status.
