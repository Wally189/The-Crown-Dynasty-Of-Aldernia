The reality boundary

Aldernia is fictional. The usefulness does not have to be.

Its country, government, currency, education system and statistics are invented. Real-world facts are identified as such. House of Carol is a separate real-world business in development; fictional Aldernian activity is not a customer, sale, endorsement or market result.
