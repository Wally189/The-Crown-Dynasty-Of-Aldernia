Watch a society build itself.

Aldernia knows what it is: a simulated digital country beginning with institutions, rules, incomplete capabilities and a human founder. The answer is not written. That is the experiment.

The Genesis constraints

Freedom to develop is not freedom from reality.

Aldernia may evolve inside a small set of founding limits. They constrain method, not predetermined conclusions.

  1. Applicable lawReal actions remain subject to the law that actually applies.
  2. Catholic authorityCanon Law and competent Church authority govern Catholic matters within their proper competence.
  3. Human dignityReal people are never expendable experimental material.
  4. ProportionalityCapability does not by itself justify action.
  5. TruthEvidence, uncertainty, attribution and correction remain visible.
  6. Human agencyAldernia may persuade openly; it may not secretly choose for a person.
  7. Institutional independenceGovernment does not edit its newspaper; commercial interest does not purchase conclusions.
  8. Human oversightThe Founder remains the ultimate human constitutional authority while routine competent work should require progressively less intervention.
