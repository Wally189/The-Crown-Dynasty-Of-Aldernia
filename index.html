The real-world boundary

A country can be fictional without being dishonest.

Aldernia treats its fictional institutions seriously. When it discusses real facts, real organisations or real commercial services, the boundary is identified.

House of Carol is Aldernia's principal transparent route into real commercial work. Independent journalism remains independent; commissioned work is labelled; real customer claims must be real.

ALDERNIA

A fictional country and participatory editorial world.

Its institutions, elections, places and in-world statistics are fictional unless a page clearly identifies real-world material. Aldernia does not claim real sovereignty, public authority, healthcare, banking, accreditation or emergency-service status.
