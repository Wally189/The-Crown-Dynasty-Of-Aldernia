Aldernian life

Harbours, libraries, markets and the small indignities of public transport.

Aldernia is intended to feel inhabited rather than administered. Ordinary life matters as much as the Crown: work, food, weather, schools, churches, sport, gardens, music, cafés, ferries, local rivalries and the institutions people actually have to use.

Public notice

Civic participation is not yet open.

The constitutional design includes a future voluntary civic layer, but this website currently creates no account, vote, citizenship, public authority or legal status. When participation exists, it must be explicitly authorised, privacy-safe and clearly fictional.

Useful explainer

What exactly is Aldernia?

Public fictional reality: Aldernia’s places, institutions, elections, history and public arguments are treated consistently in-world.

Real-world boundary: real people, businesses, purchases, evidence and external organisations are identified as real. Fiction does not manufacture endorsements, customers or authority.

The Experiment: the project asks whether institutional structure, memory, responsibility and challenge make AI-assisted work more useful. Its conclusions remain evidence-dependent.
