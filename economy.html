Banking

Private choice, public rules.

Commercial banks, mutuals, credit unions and specialists can compete. Prudential supervision sits within the central-bank framework; conduct and market behaviour are separately regulated.

  • Deposit protection: AC 100,000 per eligible person per authorised institution.
  • Temporary high balances: up to AC 1m for six months for defined life events.
  • Failure: shareholders absorb losses before taxpayer support; protected deposits and critical payments are prioritised.
  • Basic access: a low-cost/basic account standard and assisted/cash channels.

Consumer rule

Clear price. Fair value. No tricks.

Financial firms must act honestly, avoid foreseeable harm, support informed decisions and make total prices and material risks understandable.

All rates are fictional founding policy parameters and remain democratically alterable.

Work & security

Work should pay; bad luck should not become destitution.

  • Adult wage floor: AC 12.80/hour, recommended annually by an independent Pay Commission.
  • Aldernian Income Credit: means-tested support with a 50p-per-crown earnings taper above the work allowance.
  • Job Insurance: 60% of insured previous earnings up to AC 550/week for up to 26 weeks after sufficient contributions.
  • State pension: AC 12,000/year full rate at founding age 67, reviewed transparently.
  • Workplace pensions: automatic enrolment with 10% minimum total qualifying contribution.

Household prosperity test

GDP is not allowed to mark its own homework.

Aldernia also tracks median and low-income real incomes, housing costs, arrears, emergency savings, pensions, poverty, regional wages and financial distress.

Enterprise

Starting a company should be easier than keeping one alive.

The fictional Aldernian Companies Registry supports sole traders, partnerships, companies, co-operatives and community-benefit forms. Straightforward private-company incorporation is designed around a one-working-day target and an AC 80 founding fee.

Trade & productive geography

Open to trade; allergic to needless paperwork.

Merrow

Principal commercial/container port, logistics, digital services and manufacturing.

Southmarch

Freight, industrial/project cargo, ship services and precision manufacturing.

Bracken Coast

Fisheries, marine engineering, renewables and regional trade.

A single fictional trade window publishes rules and fees, accepts pre-arrival information, supports electronic payment and gives traders an appeal route. Imports are not treated as moral failure; resilience focuses on dangerous concentration and single points of failure.

Investment & regions

Not every useful investment fits a quarterly result.

The fictional Aldernian Development & Export Bank can fill evidenced gaps in SME, infrastructure and export finance. It does not take ordinary retail deposits or lend on political instruction.

Regional productivity agreements align transport, skills, housing, sites, energy and local strengths over multi-year horizons.

Additionality test

Why should public finance be here?

Every major transaction must state the market gap, private co-finance sought, public risk share, expected productive outcome, regional/distributional effect, conflicts and exit route.
