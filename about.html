About

A fictional country, operated with unreasonable seriousness.

Aldernia is a participatory editorial world: part nation-building story, part public-service media institution, part learning environment and part experiment in making useful institutions feel human.

The fiction disclosure

Aldernia is fictional. Its government, currency, elections, places, public services, statistics and institutions are invented unless a page clearly identifies real-world material.

You are welcome to suspend disbelief. We will not make the truth difficult to find.

What the project is trying to do

Inform. Explain. Educate. Scrutinise. Reflect. Connect. Entertain. Remember.

Aldernia should leave regular readers a little more informed, capable, curious, resilient and difficult to mislead—while still containing weather, football, food, local rivalries and the occasional constitutional bun dispute.

1940s grammar. 2026 interface.

The visual world borrows the clarity, confidence, economy and public-information discipline of mid-century civic communication, but not propaganda, militarism, nostalgia theatre or compulsory patriotism. The interface is modern, accessible and deliberately calm.

What it is not

Aldernia is not a sovereign state, regulator, court, bank, healthcare provider, accredited university, emergency service or diplomatic authority. Convincing worldbuilding does not create real powers.

Private machinery stays backstage

The public world is designed to stand on its own. Internal production systems, prompts, security architecture and private project machinery are not part of the public fiction.

The commercial boundary

Some useful Aldernian material may eventually lead transparently to real House of Carol services. Independent journalism, commissioned editorial and commercial material remain different categories. Fictional customers and transactions never become real-world proof.
