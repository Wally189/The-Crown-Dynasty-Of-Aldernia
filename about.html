About

A fictional country, operated with unreasonable seriousness.

Aldernia is a participatory editorial world: part nation-building story, part media institution, part public-learning experiment and part place to think about how competent institutions might feel.

The fiction disclosure

Aldernia is fictional. Its institutions, elections, places and in-world statistics are part of the fiction unless a page clearly identifies real-world material.

You are welcome to suspend disbelief. We will not make the truth hard to find.

What Aldernia does

It reports itself through The Times of Aldernia, explores culture through Aldernian Life, teaches through Common Knowledge, experiments with constitutional participation through the Electorate and uses specialist Houses to keep different kinds of expertise distinct.

What Aldernia does not claim

It is not a sovereign state, regulator, court, bank, healthcare provider, accredited university, emergency service or diplomatic authority. Fictional institutions may resemble real categories of institution because that is how worldbuilding becomes intelligible; they do not acquire real powers by being convincingly described.

The commercial boundary

Some Aldernian work may eventually connect to real House of Carol services. When that happens, the transition must be clear. Independent journalism, commissioned editorial and commercial material are different categories and should look like it.

The deeper purpose

Aldernia wants regular readers to become a little more informed, capable, curious, resilient and difficult to mislead—preferably while being entertained.
