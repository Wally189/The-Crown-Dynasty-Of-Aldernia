The Kingdom of Aldernia · in-world

A small island country with a long memory and a young constitution.

Aldernia is an island realm built around the principal island of Aldern, St Brigid’s Isle and the Kestrels. It is meant to be explored as a country first: places, institutions, culture, ordinary life and public argument.

Country profile

A place before a platform.

St Aurelia is the capital; Merrow is the largest commercial city. In-world, Aldernia is a constitutional Crown with parliamentary government and independent institutions.

The country’s public character is civilised but not sterile, principled but not preachy, traditional enough to possess memory and modern enough to repair bad systems.

The real-world boundary

Aldernia is fictional. Its future civic participation layer does not create legal citizenship, governmental authority or real public rights. Real businesses, external organisations and real-world evidence are identified as real.

Seven regions

  • 01Crown Capital Territory — St Aurelia and its immediate civic district.
  • 02Northmere — mountains, engineering, manufacturing and technical education.
  • 03Bracken Coast — western ports, fisheries, arts and exposed coastline.
  • 04Eastvale — farming, food, market towns and river settlements.
  • 05Southmarch — ports, shipyards, beaches, mixed industry and newer towns.
  • 06High Alder — forests, water, energy, outdoor life and small communities.
  • 07St Brigid’s & the Outer Isles — island communities, heritage and maritime life.
