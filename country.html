The Kingdom of Aldernia · in-world

A small island country with a long memory and a young constitution.

Aldernia is an island realm of regions, ports, mountains, farms, institutions and ordinary communities. The country is fictional; its real-world boundaries remain clearly identified.

Country profile

A place before a platform.

Aldernia is an island realm formed around the principal island of Aldern, St Brigid’s Isle and the Kestrels. St Aurelia is the capital; Merrow is the largest commercial city.

The country’s public character is intended to be civilised but not sterile, principled but not preachy, and serious about evidence without taking itself too seriously.

Constitutional form

In-world, Aldernia is a constitutional Crown with parliamentary government and independent institutions. The public civic participation layer described by the Blueprint is future functionality: no account, vote or participation mechanism is implemented by this site.

Seven regions

  • 01Crown Capital Territory — St Aurelia and its immediate civic district.
  • 02Northmere — mountains, engineering, manufacturing and technical education.
  • 03Bracken Coast — western ports, fisheries, arts and exposed coastline.
  • 04Eastvale — farming, food, market towns and river settlements.
  • 05Southmarch — ports, shipyards, beaches, mixed industry and newer towns.
  • 06High Alder — forests, water, energy, outdoor life and small communities.
  • 07St Brigid’s & the Outer Isles — island communities, heritage and maritime life.
