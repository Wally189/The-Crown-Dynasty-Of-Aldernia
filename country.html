The country

Seven regions give institutions somewhere to belong.

Aldernia’s public world is organised around a capital, working coasts, uplands, market towns, ports and islands. Geography is a stable narrative and institutional frame, not evidence of real territory.

How to read this world

Place is part of the experiment.

Institutions should acquire regional consequences, local memory and different public contexts rather than existing as placeless boxes. Real people, usage and outcomes are never invented to make those places look busy.

The Kingdom of Aldernia

Serve well. Speak plainly.

Aldernia is a fictional/simulated constitutional and institutional project. It is not a sovereign state, real public authority or regulated service provider. Real-world outcomes and revenue are separately evidenced.
