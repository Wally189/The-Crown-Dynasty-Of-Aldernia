About

What exactly is Aldernia?

A creative world, a practical laboratory and an operating environment — with some very important lines between what is fictional and what is real.

Aldernia is an online ecosystem and experiment.

It gives a collection of specialist Houses and institutions a coherent world in which to interact. The world makes complicated relationships easier to understand, test and develop than a giant diagram of AI workflows.

The world

The Crown Dynasty of Aldernia, its institutions, symbols and in-world material form the creative and constitutional setting of the experiment.

The test

The experiment asks whether structure — roles, boundaries, memory, evidence, challenge and institutional relationships — can make AI-assisted work more coherent and useful.

The real work

House of Carol is a separate real-world business. Where Aldernia discusses real-world work, it is identified as such.

The record

The Experiment Journal follows what happens as the system develops: useful discoveries, failures, repairs and the occasional idea that turns out to be less ridiculous than it first sounded.

What Aldernia is not

It is not a sovereign state, public authority, legal jurisdiction or substitute for real institutions. Fiction does not become evidence simply because it is internally consistent.

The point is not to pretend an online country is a real country. The point is to create a coherent space in which an unusual experiment can be run in public and judged by what it actually produces.
