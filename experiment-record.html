Experiment / EXP-001

EXP-001 · structured record

Evidence before mythology.

The experiment asks whether differentiated AI-assisted institutions with memory, professional boundaries, evidence rules and retained human authority produce more useful work than one undifferentiated assistant.

Current observation

The strongest supported result is bounded: specialist institutions have begun to preserve their own truth, exchange evidence, identify and repair a real cross-House defect, and stop at external evidence gates.

What is not proved

  • autonomous persistent background government;
  • national prosperity or citizen flourishing;
  • digital sovereignty or provider-independent recovery;
  • universal institutional maturity;
  • House of Carol demand, sale, customer value, repeatability or real revenue.
