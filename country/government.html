In-world constitution

Democratic settlement

Power should explain itself.

Aldernia is designed as a constitutional Crown with parliamentary government, proportional elections, professional public administration and independent institutions able to make life inconvenient for the executive when the evidence requires it.

No real civic election is live yet. The current country is public fiction. Any later real participant ballot must publish its rules, electorate, counting method and integrity controls before voting begins.

Constitution at a glance

Continuity at the top; democratic authority in the Assembly.

The Crown

King Alan is the in-world head of state. The Crown provides continuity, ceremony and narrowly defined constitutional safeguards; it does not run Government day to day or reverse elections because it dislikes the result.

The Assembly

120 elected seats in proportional multi-member areas. The Assembly legislates, approves the fictional budget, scrutinises ministers and determines who can command confidence to govern.

The Government

A Prime Minister who can command Assembly confidence leads ministers. A professional civil service converts lawful political priorities into administration while preserving evidence, legality and impartial professional advice.

The independent machinery

A democracy needs institutions whose job is occasionally to say, “No, show your workings.”

Aldernia’s constitutional design separates the institutions that govern from those that check elections, legality, spending, standards, evidence and administrative fairness. The founding architecture includes independent electoral administration, courts, audit, an ombudsman, public standards and evidence-integrity functions.

These bodies are not departments of Government and should not be asked to become helpful merely because the Government would find that convenient.

Democratic design anchors

Borrow mechanisms, not flags.

Representation

Free and equal access to political representation; elections that genuinely authorise representatives.

Rights

Civil liberties and political equality are treated as conditions of meaningful democratic choice, not optional decorations around election day.

Rule of law

Legal certainty, limits on power, equality before law and access to independent justice.

Participation

Civic life extends beyond voting: association, engagement, local democracy, consultation and a free public sphere matter too.

Elections

The ballot is a process, not a button.

The 52 planning areas are not yet approved electoral boundaries. Boundary design, island/geographic exceptions, seat distribution and the exact proportional counting formula remain matters for the independent electoral function. Before a real participatory election opens, the method must be frozen, publicly explained and not changed mid-contest.

Aldernia’s election design is benchmarked against documented international principles including equality, universality, political pluralism, transparency, accountability and the free expression of the will of electors.

Three numbers must never be muddled

6.4 million — fictional inhabitants.

Registered civic electorate — real users who later opt into an eligible participatory ballot.

Turnout — real participation in a specified vote.
