Calendar rule

Time creates context, not authority.

Scheduled items may open editorial themes, explain institutions or mark cultural events. A real election, emergency, external partnership, donation campaign or consequential public action still requires its own authority and readiness gate.

If a planned event cannot truthfully occur, the correct publication is a delay, change or cancellation — not a synthetic “success”.
