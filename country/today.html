The rhythm of the week

A quiet day is allowed to stay quiet.

The national calendar brings together civic business, cultural events and ordinary moments across fictional Aldernia. It does not turn real-world events into Aldernian news.

Plans can change. Events can be delayed or cancelled. When nothing much is happening, the country does not need a manufactured crisis to prove that it is alive.

After the founding week, a seven-day rhythm keeps the calendar moving through public affairs, places, culture, learning and everyday life — with room for less news when less news is the truthful answer.
