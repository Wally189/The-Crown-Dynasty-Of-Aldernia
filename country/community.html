Civil society

The useful space between household and state.

Clubs, charities, associations, congregations, mutual aid, heritage groups, arts organisations and sports teams make Aldernia less dependent on the assumption that every worthwhile thing requires a ministry.

All organisations described here are fictional unless explicitly identified otherwise. No real charity fundraising is active in this edition.

Voluntary Aldernia

Different reasons to show up.

Neighbourhood & mutual aid

Community kitchens, befriending, repair groups and local practical help.

Sport & youth

Local clubs, coaching, community pitches and youth activities with proper safeguarding expectations.

Culture & heritage

Choirs, local history, museums, festivals, craft and historic-place groups.

Faith communities

Worship, formation, hospitality and charitable service, while real ecclesiastical authority remains outside the fiction.

Environment

River, coast, woodland, allotment and biodiversity groups arguing over practical stewardship.

Advocacy & support

Associations can press institutions, support members and contribute evidence without being turned into extensions of Government.

Real public benefit

Future charity must remain boringly truthful about money.

Aldernia may later support genuine external charities, volunteering or public-benefit campaigns. Before that happens, the real beneficiary, legal structure, payment route, fees, data processing and distinction from personal income must be clear.

Current state: no donation button, fundraising appeal or charitable payment route is live. Fictional generosity is not a receipt.
