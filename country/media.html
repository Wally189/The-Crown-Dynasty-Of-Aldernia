Public media

News should be able to surprise the Government.

Aldernia’s public-media model is deliberately separate from Government and Palace communications. It can explain national life, praise competent work, challenge grand claims and print a correction without treating the correction as a national humiliation.

Founding newsroom status: editorial architecture is public; no consequential live-news stream is being fabricated merely to make the homepage look busy.

Editorial independence

A watchdog is not required to bark at every lamppost.

Editorial independence means evidence can produce an uncomfortable story. It also means an expected scandal is not manufactured because the newsroom would like to demonstrate courage. A claim can end as supported, unsupported, disputed, misleading, false, uncertain or genuinely unknown.

Satire is welcome. Invented damaging factual allegations presented as news are not.

Corrections desk

Current corrections

None in this public founding edition. When material factual errors are discovered, this page will identify what was wrong and what replaced it. Updates that merely add information will be labelled as updates rather than retroactively becoming “what we always meant”.
