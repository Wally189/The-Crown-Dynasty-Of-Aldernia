Statistics & provenance

A number should arrive with luggage.

Every recurring Aldernian figure should say what it is, where it came from and whether it is fictional planning data, derived worldbuilding, an observed real-user measure or an external real-world fact.

No fictional number may quietly become evidence of real audience, customers, demand, revenue or public opinion.

Reality classes

Four numbers can look identical and mean completely different things.

In-world fact

A number belonging to fictional Aldernia, such as population, area or Assembly size. It describes the world, not the website audience.

Derived worldbuilding

A planning figure calculated from established fictional premises. It remains fictional even when the arithmetic is perfectly real.

Observed participation

A real count of actual visitors or participants, if that capability is later authorised and measured. It must never be inflated with fictional population.

External fact

A real-world number imported for explanation or comparison. Its source, date, population and limitations belong beside it.

Publication rule

Definition before dashboard.

Economic series, service outcomes, election results, audience data and public-opinion measures will not be manufactured because a national website “ought” to contain charts. A series needs a definition, owner, method, time period, units, revision policy and reality classification.

If the number is real-world external data, its source and applicable population belong beside the claim. If it is fictional, the site says so.
