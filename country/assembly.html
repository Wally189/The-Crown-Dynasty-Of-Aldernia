Assembly docket

Disagreement is a feature, not a software defect.

The founding Assembly page shows how Aldernian institutions can disagree without the website deciding which political choice a visitor should make.

No real vote is open. These are fictional issue briefs, not party manifestos, polling, electoral predictions or recommendations to real voters.

Questions before the House

Trade-offs with the comforting bits removed.

One national rail fare?

Case for: simpler journeys, easier interchange and clearer national access.

Challenge: a uniform model can shift costs between regions and weaken local flexibility.

Evidence needed: passenger patterns, subsidy, distributional effects, operator capacity and accessibility.

East Aure housing growth?

Case for: more housing near jobs and existing urban infrastructure.

Challenge: flood risk, transport capacity, habitat and public-service demand may make nominal supply expensive elsewhere.

Evidence needed: housing need, land, infrastructure, flood/environment assessment and delivery capacity.

Kestrels ferry guarantee?

Case for: island access is essential infrastructure, not merely a commercial route.

Challenge: minimum-service guarantees create real capacity and subsidy obligations.

Evidence needed: passenger/freight demand, resilience, cost, weather reliability and island equality.

National library floor?

Case for: minimum access protects a common knowledge infrastructure.

Challenge: national prescriptions can constrain local priorities and service design.

Evidence needed: access, usage, local need, cost and alternatives — without treating footfall alone as public value.

How debate works

Evidence does not abolish politics.

Evidence can reveal likely consequences, uncertainty, cost and who bears them. It does not automatically decide how competing legitimate values should be weighted. Ministers, representatives, independent institutions and citizens therefore retain different roles.

The public-data and media functions may fact-check factual claims. They do not award an overall “winner” to a political position.
