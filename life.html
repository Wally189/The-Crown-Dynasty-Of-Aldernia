St Brigid’s Isle · The September Interruption

Twelve extension leads and a pot of soup.

When power and mobile coverage became unreliable, nobody appointed the old library as an information hub. People simply kept arriving there.

The librarian had a battery radio. The café opposite had a gas hob. St Brigid’s parish hall had folding tables, spare blankets and the useful habit of knowing who lived alone. By lunchtime, a handwritten board by the library door listed ferry cancellations, charging times and the latest verified public notices.

Somebody brought soup. Somebody else brought a rumour about contaminated water. The rumour was crossed out, dated and replaced with the correction once the source had been checked.

There were arguments about whether St Aurelia had forgotten the islands, complaints about mobile coverage, three unsolicited theories about undersea cables and one elderly resident who observed that the outage had finally made everybody stop looking at their phones.
