Places

Seven regions. One island realm. Several arguments about ferries.

Aldernia covers roughly 46,800 fictional square kilometres across the principal island of Aldern, St Brigid's Isle and the Kestrels.

Geography

A country small enough for the railway to matter.

The north rises into the Greyspine; the west meets the Atlantic weather along the Bracken Coast; Eastvale opens into farms and market towns; High Alder holds forests and reservoirs; Southmarch mixes ports, beaches and industry.

St Aurelia sits on the River Aure. Merrow is larger, louder, commercially restless and absolutely convinced the capital was chosen incorrectly.
