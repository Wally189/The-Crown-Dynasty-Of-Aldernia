House of Vivienne — Banking

Finance expertise, with the boundary left visible.

Vivienne is Aldernia’s governed simulated finance-and-banking institution. It is deliberately broader than a retail bank: its remit spans banking, payments, treasury, markets, investments, insurance, pensions, accounting, tax, public finance, risk, conduct, financial crime, customer operations, technology, data, audit and financial education.

The public claim is deliberately narrower than the ambition: selected bounded internal capabilities have been demonstrated. A real bank, regulated provider or whole-House mature institution has not.

The Aldernian role

Support the kingdom without taking it over.

Vivienne supplies specialist financial judgement where money, banking, economic evidence, financial resilience or financial-system questions materially arise.

Government, business, education, health and other Aldernian institutions retain their own mandates. Consulting Vivienne does not transfer their decisions to Vivienne, and Vivienne does not rewrite another institution’s specialist conclusion merely to make the national story tidier.

  1. Public moneyBudget, finance and economic evidence within the fictional state, with specialist ownership preserved.
  2. BusinessUnit economics, cash, cost, forecast and resilience analysis when real evidence exists.
  3. HouseholdsFinancial education, budgeting, borrowing, scams and resilience information without pretending to give regulated personal advice.
  4. SystemsBanking, payments, markets, risk and financial-system analysis inside the governed simulation.

Post-Genesis evidence

Autonomy includes correcting yourself.

Vivienne’s first published post-Genesis evidence is deliberately ordinary: keeping decision information consistent with the source record.

The Founder first had to correct an execution failure in the runtime. After that rescue, Vivienne continued under existing authority and independently located a real internal control defect: the official-data snapshot had been refreshed, but the downstream Finance MI still carried stale source periods and a stale CPI figure.

Vivienne corrected the decision-facing MI, recorded the defect and added a regression test so the same propagation failure is explicitly checked in future. This is counted in the Observatory as one autonomous completion and one correction. The Founder intervention is separately counted as one rescue.

Why this matters

Autonomy is not “never needing a human.” It is the ability to perform competent work inside existing authority, preserve STOPs and return accurate state without requiring the Founder to diagnose each task.

Real-world boundary

House of Vivienne is not a real bank.

The fiction may be convincing; the boundary may not be hidden.

NO LICENCE CLAIMVivienne’s controlled records do not evidence a real banking licence or FCA/PRA permission.
NO DEPOSITSIt does not accept real customer deposits or claim deposit-protection coverage.
NO PAYMENTSIt does not execute real customer payment services or move funds as an Aldernian bank.
NO ADVICE CLAIMIt does not present this public profile as regulated personal financial, investment, insurance or pension advice.
NO MATURITY INFLATIONSelected internal capability does not justify describing the whole institution as ASSURED, OPERATING or MATURE.
NO FICTIONAL PROOFSimulated transactions, rehearsals and fictional Aldernian economics are not evidence of real customers, permissions or market activity.

Capability state

Commissioned where proved. Open where not.

Vivienne’s internal maturity model moves from discovery through commissioning, assurance, operation and maturity. Higher labels require their own evidence.

DISCOVERED→ARCHITECTED→CONSTITUTED→PROFESSIONALLY BASELINED→PROCEDURALISED→COMMISSIONED→ASSURED→OPERATING→MATURE

Current public description: selected bounded internal capabilities are commissioned and tested; genuine independent assurance, repeated whole-House operation and mature resilience remain unearned.
