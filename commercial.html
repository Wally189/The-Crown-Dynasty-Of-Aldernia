The real-world boundary

Useful ideas may lead to real work. The crossing should be obvious.

House of Carol is a separate real-world business in development. Aldernia may explain problems and demonstrate thinking; it does not manufacture Carol customers, revenue or endorsements.

Current state

No live commercial transaction route.

No checkout, payment collection, lead form, sponsorship market or advertising programme is activated by this site.

The reader should always be able to tell

  • Is this independent journalism?
  • Is this commissioned editorial?
  • Is this advertising or sponsorship?
  • Is this a real commercial service?
  • Is a customer result real and evidenced, or fictional illustration?

Rule

Carol may commission production. It may not commission truth.

Fear, fake endorsements, invented partnerships, fictional revenue and disguised marketing are incompatible with Aldernia's public promise.

Fiction is not economic proof.

A fictional Aldernian business, vote, reader, transaction or partnership does not count as House of Carol demand, market validation, revenue or customer evidence. Any future real-world Carol offer must identify itself clearly at the point the reader leaves the fictional/editorial environment.
