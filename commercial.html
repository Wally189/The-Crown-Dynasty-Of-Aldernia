The real-world boundary

Useful ideas may lead to real work. The boundary should never be hidden.

House of Carol is Aldernia's commercial bridge. Independent journalism can explain a problem; a clearly identified commercial route may then offer real help.

What the reader should always be able to tell

  • Is this independent journalism?
  • Is this commissioned editorial?
  • Is this advertising or sponsorship?
  • Is this a real commercial service?
  • Is a customer result real and evidenced, or merely fictional illustration?

Rule

Carol may commission production. It may not commission truth.

Fear, fake endorsements, fabricated customer stories and disguised marketing are incompatible with Aldernia's public promise.

Current site state

This Aldernia repository establishes the commercial boundary but does not operate a live checkout, payment flow, sponsorship programme or public advertising market.

Any real House of Carol service promoted from Aldernia must identify itself as real-world commercial material at the point the reader crosses from the fictional/editorial environment.

House of Carol may operate real-world commercial services outside the fictional country. Commercial status must be clear wherever that boundary is crossed.
