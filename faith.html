Faith & public life

Catholic in inheritance. Free in conscience.

Aldernia gives Catholic faith, moral thought, worship, history and social teaching a visible place in national life. It is equally clear about who possesses real Church authority — and who does not.

Authority

Who speaks for the Catholic Church?

House of Catholic does not exercise the Catholic Magisterium. It researches, verifies, explains and routes Catholic questions to the real authorities that do.

In the real Catholic Church, the teaching office belongs to the Roman Pontiff and the bishops in communion with him, exercised according to Catholic doctrine and canon law. Individual diocesan bishops are authentic teachers for the faithful entrusted to them. Roman dicasteries assist the Pope and bishops within their proper competences.

Checked 20 September 2026: the Roman Pontiff is Pope Leo XIV, the 267th Pope of the Catholic Church.

Aldernia does not invent a fictional bishop, diocese, episcopal conference, imprimatur, dispensation or ecclesiastical approval and then treat it as real Catholic authority.

House of Catholic

A founding institution, not a decorative chapel.

The House works across Aldernia whenever a genuine Catholic doctrinal, moral, canonical, liturgical, educational, safeguarding, bioethical, social, interreligious, heritage, charity or technology question appears.

Doctrine & authority

Government & public ethics

Catholic social thought may challenge power without becoming Government.

Human dignity, the common good, solidarity, subsidiarity, religious freedom, care for vulnerable people and the dignity of work inform Catholic specialist challenge. Government, Parliament, courts and independent institutions retain their own constitutional competence.

Education

Parents matter; Catholic status cannot be invented.

Aldernian education recognises parents as primary educators while preserving the child's rights, safeguarding and national educational entitlement. A fictional faith-school ethos does not create real Catholic-school status.

Health & bioethics

Clinical truth and Catholic moral truth remain distinct competences.

House of Nathaniel owns health and clinical evidence. House of Catholic supplies Catholic moral and bioethical research where genuinely engaged. Neither substitutes for the other.

Media & truth

Accurate Catholic sourcing without editorial capture.

House of Josie remains editorially independent. House of Catholic can correct doctrinal misstatement and establish source provenance; it cannot dictate a favourable editorial conclusion.

Technology & AI

Human responsibility does not disappear into the machine.

Aldernia's AI-native institutions require identifiable human responsibility for consequential decisions, proportionate challenge and remedy, respect for privacy and truth, and human primacy over artificial systems.

Current founding assurance

The realm has been checked — and one material gap was corrected.

The current founding baseline is not given a blanket ecclesiastical “compliance certificate”. Catholic assurance is continuous and source-specific.

The current public settlement is materially aligned with Catholic principles on religious freedom and non-coercion, equal human dignity, the common good, solidarity and subsidiarity, the proper distinction of Church and political community, the dignity of work, care for vulnerable people and responsible stewardship.

A material AI-governance gap was identified during the founding review. The common Aldernian AI controls now require clear human responsibility, challenge and remedy for consequential AI-assisted decisions, and prohibit entrusting lethal or otherwise irreversible decisions to artificial systems.

The House's own use of the name House of Catholic remains subject to an unresolved Canon 216 real-authority question. Aldernia therefore makes no claim that the House is an organ, association or institution approved, erected or authorised by the Catholic Church.
