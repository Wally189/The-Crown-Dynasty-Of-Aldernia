Privacy

Very little data because very little needs collecting.

Aldernia is deliberately static and currently introduces no participation or tracking technology.

The current site contains no forms, user accounts, analytics, advertising, behavioural profiling, marketing pixels, local storage or non-essential first-party cookies.

The pages do not ask visitors to provide personal information. Hosting and network services used to deliver the site may necessarily process technical connection information for delivery, security and reliability under their own terms and policies.

Civic registration, voting, forms, analytics, advertising or other features that collect personal information or store or access information on a visitor’s device are not active. Any future activation would require separate current privacy, security, legal and safeguarding review.
