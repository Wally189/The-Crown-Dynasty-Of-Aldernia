Privacy

Very little data because very little needs collecting.

The review candidate is deliberately static and introduces no participation or tracking technology.

This candidate contains no forms, user accounts, analytics, advertising, behavioural profiling, marketing pixels, client-side JavaScript, local storage or non-essential first-party cookies.

The pages do not ask visitors to provide personal information. If later hosted publicly, the hosting and network services used to deliver the website may necessarily process technical connection information for delivery, security and reliability under their own terms and policies.

Civic registration, voting, forms, analytics, advertising or other features that collect personal information or store or access information on a visitor’s device remain outside this candidate. They require separate current privacy, security, legal and safeguarding review before release.
