Privacy

This static build does not operate a civic register.

The present repository contains a static public-world website. The demo electoral interaction runs locally in the browser and is not designed to transmit or count a vote.

No live electorate, newsletter, supporter membership, advertising profile, payment route or analytics programme is established by this build.

Before any future registration or voting feature opens, Aldernia must publish the purpose, data required, retention, access, security, complaints route and other applicable privacy information for that specific feature.
