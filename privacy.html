Privacy baseline

Observe the system, not the visitor.

Genesis deliberately begins without accounts, registration, forms, advertising profiles or first-party behavioural analytics.

What Aldernia currently collects

The public Genesis pages contain no Aldernia-operated form, login, vote, payment field, newsletter signup or analytics script.

Hosting

The site is delivered through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process ordinary technical request data under its own service and privacy arrangements; Aldernia does not claim control over provider-level logging.

Future changes

If the experiment later earns a reason to collect personal information, the purpose, necessity, data required, retention, access, security and user rights must be designed and disclosed before collection begins.

The rule

Do not collect information merely because technology makes collection convenient.
