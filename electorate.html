Constitution Lab

What makes participation real?

  • One eligible civic account, one vote in the relevant ballot.
  • Secret ballot: individual choices are not published.
  • Rules, timetable and counting method frozen before voting opens.
  • Independent administration outside Government and contestants.
  • Transparent turnout, valid-vote totals and result method.
  • No political microtargeting based on inferred personal preferences.
  • No quiet substitution of an institutional favourite when the electorate chooses differently.

A demonstration is not an election.
