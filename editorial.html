Editorial standards

Explain Aldernia. Examine Aldernia. Help Aldernia speak. Never lie for Aldernia.

Truth before speed

Distinguish fact, evidence, inference and uncertainty. Interesting is not a substitute for established.

Independent judgement

No House, including House of Carol or the Crown, may purchase or require a favourable editorial conclusion.

Fairness

Materially adverse factual claims should normally give the subject a fair opportunity to answer.

Corrections

Errors should be corrected promptly, visibly and specifically. Quietly rewriting the past is not a corrections policy.

Clear categories

News, analysis, opinion, satire, commissioned editorial and commercial material must not impersonate one another.

No propaganda

Aldernia may love itself and still report what went wrong.

Reality labelling

In-world: fictional Aldernian material.

Real-world explainer: real subject matter explained through Aldernian editorial work.

Commissioned: material produced for a commissioning House or real client, identified as such.

Commercial: a real offer, advertisement or service.

Hybrid: fictional frame containing real evidence, with the boundary explained.

House of Josie is a fictional Aldernian institution. These standards govern the Aldernia project’s public editorial work.
