Accessibility

Accessibility grows with the country.

Aldernia aims to follow WCAG 2.2 AA design practice and treats accessibility as part of ordinary design and quality assurance rather than a final decoration.

The site uses semantic HTML, skip navigation, keyboard-operable native controls, visible focus indicators, responsive layouts, text rather than colour alone for meaning, reduced-motion support and no essential dependence on JavaScript.

Navigation controls are designed around generous pointer targets, the national layouts reflow for small screens, and the current palette uses accessible foreground/background contrast for ordinary text.

The site does not claim independent accessibility certification. Accessibility is checked during release assurance, including keyboard operation, zoom and reflow, focus visibility, colour contrast and representative assistive-technology sampling.
