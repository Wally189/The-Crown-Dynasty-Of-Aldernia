Accessibility

This candidate is deliberately simple: semantic HTML, keyboard-operable links, visible focus states, responsive layouts, meaningful alternative text for the two symbols, no motion-dependent content and no colour-only navigation.

It is designed with WCAG 2.2 AA principles in mind, but no claim of formal or independent conformance is made at this candidate stage.

If the site grows beyond this small gateway, accessibility testing should grow with it rather than being bolted on afterwards.
