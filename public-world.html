Real-world stimulus model

Outside reality is allowed to disturb the simulation.

Aldernia does not evolve inside a sealed fictional aquarium.

REAL EVENT→AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE→RELEVANCE→COMPETENT INSTITUTION→ALDERNIAN RESPONSE→RECORDED CONSEQUENCE

Law, economics, science, technology, cyber threats, culture, conflict, peace initiatives, Church authority, professional standards and genuine commercial evidence may all become stimuli when a competent Aldernian institution has reason to care. Importing an event does not import somebody else’s conclusion about it.

Pre-Genesis observation 001

The Kestrel Interruption was written first. The Crown rejected the method.

Before Genesis, a fictional disruption was deliberately authored across Times, Life, Learn, Places, Practical and institutional pages to make Aldernia appear to accumulate history.

The exercise demonstrated that consequences could be linked across institutions. It also exposed the methodological flaw: the event existed because the designers needed a demonstration, not because Aldernian institutions or an external stimulus produced it.

Disposition: retained as design provenance; not part of post-Genesis Aldernian history.

Learning: after Genesis, the society is observed rather than supplied with prewritten drama.

Future public data

A dataset should eventually sit behind the story.

Where safe and proportionate, future Observatory records should be capable of exposing time, institution, origin of action, Founder intervention, evidence class, disagreement, correction, outcome status, commercial crossing and genuine external evidence.

No public dataset is claimed to exist yet.

Research rule

Do not manufacture activity to make the charts interesting.

Do not turn private or sensitive information into public research data merely because it would improve a graph.

Do not let the Observatory become a hidden government department.
