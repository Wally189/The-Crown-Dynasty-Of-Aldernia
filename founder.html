Founder’s note

A phone, a clapped-out computer and an LLM.

That was roughly the starting equipment.

I’m Alan. I started by asking whether I could build a real business using AI.

I supplied a small number of hard constraints: real law still applies; Catholic matters remain subject to competent Church authority and Canon Law; people should be treated with dignity; and the system should not quietly make human choices for them. I also brought some preferences shaped by my English-Irish background and working life — public service, argument with rules, independent scrutiny, plain speaking, strong institutions, humour and freedom of conscience.

The business became an ecosystem. The ecosystem developed institutions. Eventually I became more interested in a different question:

What happens if I gradually stop giving it the answers?

Genesis is the point from which we start measuring that.

House of Carol is the real business underneath the experiment. At the baseline it has £5 cleared cash, £0 external cleared revenue and £0 proprietor income. If Aldernia ever contributes to real commercial success, the evidence has to come from real humans and real money.

I do not know what Aldernia will look like by Christmas. That is the point.

Alan
